Help

A class says "could not place"

Check three things: an instructor is ticked for that subject, that instructor has hours left, and a room is free during the section's hours.

How do I make night classes?

On Sections, choose Night class for that section. Use Customize to pick its days and hours.

Build schedule didn't change my old classes

It only fills classes that have no time yet. Delete a class on the Schedule page, then build again.

Can I edit a class by hand?

Yes. On Schedule, press Edit on any class, or open the Weekly grid and click one. Conflicts are checked for you.

How do students see it?

Open Student view in the left menu and share that link. No account needed.
